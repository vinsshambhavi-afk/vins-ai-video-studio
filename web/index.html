Your scenes will appear here.

3. Export and render

The browser prepares a ZIP containing your script, scene plan, photos, logo and music. Google Colab renders the MP4 in the cloud. No GPU is needed on your PC.

After downloading both files, open the Colab notebook link in README, upload the notebook to Colab, run its cells and upload your project ZIP when prompted.
